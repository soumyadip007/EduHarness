# IRB / Ethics Application Draft (EduHarness Pilot)

## Study Title
Evaluating a Pedagogical Harness for Trustworthy AI Tutoring in Undergraduate Python.

## Objectives
- Measure learning gain via pre/post tests.
- Measure teacher intervention burden and governance effectiveness.
- Compare H2 vs H3 conditions in a controlled pilot.

## Participants
- 20–40 undergraduate students.
- 1–3 instructors/TAs.

## Design
- 2–4 weeks, two-group or within-subject setup.
- Group A: H2; Group B: H3.

## Data Collected
- Chat traces, scaffold levels, escalations, policy patches.
- Pre/post test scores.
- Teacher and student survey responses.

## Privacy
- Random IDs; no direct identifiers in exports.
- Raw logs stored locally and access-restricted.

## Consent
- Informed consent required for all participants.
- Right to withdraw at any time.
